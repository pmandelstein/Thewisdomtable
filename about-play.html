About the table.

A gift, freely given

The Wisdom Table is a simple place to bring a real question and hear how history's wisest voices might answer it, side by side. Socrates next to a hospice nurse. The Buddha next to Maya Angelou.

How it works

You ask a question and choose who sits at your table. Each reply is imagined with the help of AI, drawing on what that person actually taught and how they spoke. Think of it as an invitation to go and read the real words. It's a place for reflection, not a substitute for a counselor or the people who love you.

Your privacy

There's nothing to sign up for, and we don't ask who you are. To write the answers, your question is sent to an AI service, so please leave out names and private details you wouldn't want shared. Answers aren't saved, so share or copy any you'd like to keep.

Who set the table

Paul Mandelstein is an author, publisher, philosopher and grandfather. He co-founded The Farm community in Tennessee, founded The Book Publishing Company and Quantum Publishing, and authored several new mythology stories, including Small Wisdoms, 128 fables distilled from a lifetime. An ordained Soto Zen practitioner in Suzuki Roshi's lineage, he was a workshop facilitator at Esalen Institute.

The Wisdom Table is a project of Liminal Arts. More at paulmandelstein.com.
